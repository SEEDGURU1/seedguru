About Us

Golden Bio Science is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company. Under the Seed Guru brand, we clean, test and pack seeds and supply them to our dealers and shopkeepers, from where farmers buy them.

Our goal is simple: farmers should get seeds that germinate well, give a good harvest and are worth every rupee. Our philosophy is "Best to Better".
